Privacy Policy & Data Security Commitment — Story Audio

Story Audio is a free app for listening to public-domain audio stories. We respect your privacy and commit to the following:

1. No Server Storage (Offline First)

Listening history, Saved list, and Downloads stay on your device. We do not run a cloud account database to store your personal library.

2. No Login / No Personal Profile

We do not require name, email, phone number, or account signup to listen. We do not collect contacts or photos for app features.

3. Advertising

The app may show ads to stay free. We do not sell your personal notes or listening history as a data product. Ads, if shown, are provided by Google AdMob and are not based on a personal profile we create about you.

4. Audio content sources (copyright)

Story audio files come from public-domain catalogs hosted on Internet Archive, including LibriVox volunteer recordings released into the public domain. Story Audio is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially connected to LibriVox or Internet Archive.

5. Video tab

Videos open/play through YouTube. Those videos remain owned by their uploaders under YouTube terms. Story Audio does not claim ownership of YouTube content.

6. Minimal permissions

The app only requests permissions needed for audio playback, offline saving, and related features when you use them. We do not access unrelated personal files on your device.

7. Contact / takedown

If you believe specific content should not appear in this app, contact us by email or through the support channel on the App Store or Google Play listing for the store where you installed Story Audio. We will review promptly.

In short: Story Audio is built to help you listen to free public-domain stories. Your library data stays on your device. We do not require an account, and we do not sell your listening history.
